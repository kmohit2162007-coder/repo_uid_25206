One Piece is a world-renowned manga series by Eiichiro Oda that follows Monkey D. Luffy and his crew, the Straw Hat Pirates, in their search for the ultimate treasure to crown him the next King of the Pirates. As of late 2025, the series has over 110 compiled volumes and is the best-selling manga in history
