Welcome to One Piece Anime

This is the anime section of our One Piece website.

One Piece is a world-renowned anime series by Eiichiro Oda that follows Monkey D. Luffy and his crew, the Straw Hat Pirates, in their search for the ultimate treasure to crown him the next King of the Pirates. As of late 2025, the series has over 1000 episodes and is one of the most popular anime in history.
