Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, commonly known as Amrita University,

multi-disciplinary private deemed university headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

SRM university

SRM Institute of Science and Technology, formerly known as SRM University,
is a private deemed university located in Kattankulathur, Tamil Nadu, India.
Established in 1985, it has grown to become one of the largest private universities in India,
offering a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across various disciplines.

VIT university

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) is a private deemed university located in Vellore, Tamil Nadu, India. Established in 1984, VIT has gained a reputation for its academic excellence, research contributions, and industry collaborations. The university offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across various disciplines, including engineering, technology, management, science, and humanities.
